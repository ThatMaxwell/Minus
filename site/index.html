Computer use

Fast hands.
Careful judgment.

Minus agents use real apps the way you do: they click, type and move between windows quickly. Every action is logged, and you can watch live or take over.

  • Works in any app or website, no integrations needed
  • Stops and asks before anything it can’t undo
  • Every click and keystroke is in the log

Routines

Show it once. It runs on its own.

Walk an agent through a task one time. It saves it as a routine and runs it on schedule, every week.

Approvals

You stay in charge.

Agents ask before anything they can’t undo, like sending email or spending money. Decide what each one may do on its own.

  • Approve, edit or reject from anywhere
  • Set limits per agent and per app
  • Your keys and files stay yours
